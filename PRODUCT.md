# Product

## Register

product

## Users
YouTube Content Creators (50%), Indie Makers (25%), Consultants/Analysts (15%), Researchers (5%), Product Managers (5%). They use this system to synthesize video content into comprehensive intelligence reports.

## Product Purpose
YouTube Video Content Intelligence Platform to synthesize video transcript data into structured intelligence dimensions (UCIS v5.1).

## Brand Personality
Obsidian-Escher visual theme: Pure black (#000000) background, Electric Cyan accents, flat borders, high asymmetry, mathematical precision.

## Anti-references
SaaS-cream palettes, rounded corners, bouncy easing, glassmorphism, flat-scale typography, emoji icons.

## Design Principles
1. Obsidian-Escher Aesthetic (Recursive Tessellation, zero rounded corners)
2. Persona-Weighted UI Density (70/30 Revelatory/Contemplative UX split)
3. Insight Signaling via Electric Cyan vs Pure White accents
4. High Contrast and Accessibility (WCAG AAA readability)

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AAA readability on black, high contrast, support for screen readers, keyboard-only traversal, no color-only information coding.
