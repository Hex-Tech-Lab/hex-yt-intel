The Unit Economics Miracle

Strategy must pivot immediately from cost-containment to aggressive user acquisition.

Shift: v1 debated pricing in the abstract. v2 discovered through codebase verification that the checkout system is hardcoded to $9/mo. Because of the COGS miracle, this is actually a viable feature, not a fatal bug. Delta: Decide whether to keep $9/mo for acquisition or enforce $49/mo for margin leverage.

Shift: v1 feared IP blocks from raw scraping. v2 knows Decodo handles the scraping, but identified a Single Point of Failure (no fallback transcript provider built). Also, v2 discovered the app is locked to Haiku (`COMMERCIAL_TRIAL_MODE`) and the RAG tables are completely empty.
