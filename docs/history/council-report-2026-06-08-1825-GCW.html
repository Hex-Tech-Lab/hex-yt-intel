Date: 2026-06-08 | Focus: $0-Runway Survival

Executive Summary (The Hard Truth)

The council fundamentally rejects the premise of an "8-week launch strategy." With $0 runway, 8 weeks is a luxury you do not have. This is an existence problem, not a marketing optimization problem. The core constraints—no cash, no legal entity, reliance on vulnerable 3rd party APIs—mean that velocity to first revenue is the only metric that matters. Furthermore, the council universally identified critical technical oversights: the application is currently non-functional (missing API keys and DB schemas), and the underlying transcript scraping pipeline will likely trigger immediate IP blocks or violate YouTube API ToS if scaled.

The Fatal Flaws (Highest-Rated Risks)

  • The Legal/Platform Guillotine (Response E): Commercializing scraped YouTube transcripts without a corporate shield invites unlimited personal liability. A DMCA takedown or API revocation kills the business overnight.
  • Technical Fragility (Identified by all Peer Reviewers): YouTube actively IP-blocks unauthenticated server-side scraping. Building a business model that assumes infinite free scraping will break the application on day one. Additionally, generating 11-dimension outputs will likely exceed Vercel execution timeouts.
  • The Non-Functional Codebase: As identified in the peer reviews, the production DB tables (embeddings, videos, playlists) are empty, and OPENROUTER_API_KEY is missing. The engine cannot currently generate a summary.
  • MoR Underwriting Risk: Dodo/Paddle heavily scrutinize AI wrappers in copyright gray areas. An unincorporated Egyptian founder selling derivative works faces a high risk of application rejection or account freezes.

The Winning Strategy (The Specific Plan)

The "Survival Arithmetic" Protocol (Response G + D Synthesis)

Cancel all unnecessary dev subscriptions immediately to stop the bleeding. Do not attempt US infrastructure migrations or complex feature additions. Your objective is to validate willingness-to-pay manually before writing another line of code. Secure a Merchant of Record (Paddle/Dodo), publish basic legal templates, and attempt to pre-sell $39 packages to 5-10 users via direct outreach. If you cannot secure 5 manual sales, the product hypothesis is dead, and the 8-week plan is moot.
